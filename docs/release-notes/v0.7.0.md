New interface: four tabs (Extract, Gallery, Look, Output), Preferences (Ctrl+,), a search for every command and setting (Ctrl+K) and the list of shortcuts (?). Three skins, switchable in Preferences: Studio, Atomic and Mission; the viewer and the scopes stay neutral in all of them. Fixes: marking in or out (I, O) or capturing (C) right after a frame step no longer risks taking the previous frame; shortcuts no longer stop working after a click on a checkbox or the timeline; editing the look no longer reruns FFmpeg on every change; the status bar now names the decoder the analysis really used (GPU or processor).
