New interface: four tabs (Extract, Gallery, Look, Output), Preferences (Ctrl+,), a search for every command and setting (Ctrl+K) and the list of shortcuts (?). Three skins, switchable in Preferences: Studio, Atomic and Mission; the viewer and the scopes stay neutral in all of them. In Studio, the accent color is yours to choose (gold by default). Selected buttons and tabs are outlined in the accent color. Larger transport with a big timecode, and a mute button (M). In Extract, the shot settings fold away so the shot list comes first. With the export preview on, moving through the film is instant: the frame follows at once, then the exact one replaces it. Contact sheets: a PDF is always made of pages (Screen 150 dpi or Print 300 dpi), frames follow the film's order, and a single image that would be too large is split instead of exhausting memory. If the interface ever stops, Photogramme now says so and offers to reload, instead of a black window. Fixes: J/K/L reverse playback that could not be stopped; marking in or out (I, O) or capturing (C) right after a frame step no longer risks taking the previous frame; shortcuts no longer stop working after a click on a checkbox or the timeline; editing the look no longer reruns FFmpeg on every change; the status bar now names the decoder the analysis really used (GPU or processor).
